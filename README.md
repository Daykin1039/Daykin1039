### Featured Portfolio Projects
These are case studies based on the most recent types of scenarios I have worked on professionally and the hypotheses explored in each. The descriptions below do not include employer data, internal systems, supplier information, or confidential business rules. 

1. Pricing Change & Sales Impact Analysis
Focus: SQL · Historical sales · Pricing analytics · Business recommendations
Analyze product-level sales before and after a price change, with the option to segment results by branch, product group, or other relevant business dimensions.
- How did sales change after a price update?
- Are results consistent across products or locations?
- What data-quality checks are needed before drawing conclusions?

2. Product Data Quality Audit
Focus: Data validation · SQL · Exception analysis · Root-cause investigation
Create repeatable checks for duplicate records, missing or invalid attributes, inconsistent dimensions, and other product-data issues that may affect search, product pages, or downstream reporting.
- Which records fail defined quality checks?
- Which issues are most important to resolve first?
- How can exceptions be grouped and tracked for follow-up?

3. Catalog Completeness & E-commerce Readiness
Focus: Product information · Completeness metrics · Reporting · Visualization
Build a sample analysis of catalog completeness across product attributes, descriptions, images, and other enrichment fields, then explore how completeness could be monitored over time.
- Where are the largest gaps in product information?
- How do completeness and enrichment rates vary by product group?
- What measures would help teams prioritize improvements?

4. Supplier Scorecard & Performance Reporting
Focus: KPI design · Data reconciliation · Power BI concepts · Stakeholder reporting
Develop a sample scorecard that summarizes catalog or data-quality performance and makes results easier to compare, explain, and act on.
- Which measures best communicate data quality and completeness?
- How should results differ for all products versus actively selling products?
- How can scorecards support constructive, evidence-based supplier discussions?

5. Large-Scale Data Validation & Reconciliation
Focus: SQL · Data loading · Record validation · Integration analysis
Demonstrate a structured approach to checking data across source files, staging areas, and reporting datasets. My professional experience includes reviewing automotive catalog interchange data and fitment datasets at a substantial scale, including an almost 100-million-record ACES data load and PIES files containing millions of interchange records.
- Do record counts and key fields reconcile across stages?
- Which issues may stem from formatting, missing records, duplicates, or transformation rules?
- How can validation results be documented for technical and business teams?

6. Purchase Patterns & Customer Inactivity Signals
Focus: SQL · Python · Exploratory analysis · Percentiles
Use synthetic transaction history to explore purchase intervals and identify unusually long periods of inactivity. Compare percentile-based thresholds while accounting for customers with limited transaction history.
- What is a typical interval between purchases?
- How might P90 and P95 thresholds flag potential inactivity?
- What limitations should be considered before treating a signal as churn?
